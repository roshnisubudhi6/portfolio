The problem

General chatbots don't know your documents, and building a bot that answers from them usually means wiring up loaders, embeddings and a vector store by hand.

How it works

Point it at a file in Google Drive: it's split into chunks, embedded with OpenAI and stored in Supabase. Then chat — an AI agent searches those vectors, remembers the conversation in Postgres and answers from your file. The sample bot knows a simplified Rules of Golf.

Demo

Index the PDF once, then ask. Each answer cites the rule it came from, and the follow-up question ("And if it were match play?") uses chat memory to fill in the context. Open as GIF ↗
